Method

Step one

Collect the filings

Scripts pull sworn qualifying papers, ethics disclosures, Sunbiz registrations, property and campaign finance records, lobbyist registries, council agendas and minutes, and keep a copy of every page.

Step two

Match and cross-reference

Names and addresses are matched only on an exact key, never a guess. Where two records touch — a donor who is also a vendor, a treasurer who works for an applicant — that becomes a lead to check.

Step three

Publish with the source

Nothing is published by hand. A fact with no record behind it does not appear, and every row on this site links back to the filing it was read from.

PoliTrak is built entirely from public records: sworn qualifying oaths, state ethics disclosures, Sunbiz corporate filings, county property and voter rolls, campaign finance reports, lobbyist registrations, and Village council minutes and agendas. Nothing here comes from rumor, anonymous tips, or private data.

Some people appear with a limited profile — name, occupation and city only. That's how private individuals show up when a public record (a contribution, a permit, a court filing) names them but they aren't a candidate, an official, or someone acting in an official capacity. Their home address and other personal details are never published.

Spot an error, a missing source, or something that should be corrected? Open an issue on the project's repository describing what's wrong and, where you can, a citation for the correct fact.
